Questions

The ones that actually come up.

I already have a dictation app — why do I need this too?
Because owning it isn't the same as using it. Most people who buy a dictation app end up reaching for it a fraction of the time they meant to, then fall back into typing out of habit. TalkRemind is the nudge that closes that gap — it doesn't replace your dictation app, it makes sure you actually open it.
Does it read what I'm typing?
No. TalkRemind only observes keystroke timing through the macOS Accessibility API — when you type and how much, never the characters themselves.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
The nudges stop. Recording keeps running for free — your dictation share, trend, and time saved stay visible so you can see the effect wear off in your own numbers before deciding to buy.
Which dictation apps does it work with?
Any app you trigger with a hotkey — Wispr Flow, superwhisper, VoiceInk, Aqua Voice, macOS's built-in Dictation, and others. TalkRemind shows that app's own hotkey in the nudge, it doesn't replace your dictation app.
Can I turn it off for specific apps or times?
Yes. Exempt individual apps permanently, set a quiet-hours window, or pause the whole thing for 30 minutes, 2 hours, until evening, or indefinitely from the menu bar.
Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Typing and dictation events stay on your Mac. The only network calls are license validation and update checks — no analytics, no tracking.
